The sun needs a nap. Collect all the ice cubes to let the sun set!

  • W/A/S/D — move forward/left/back/right
  • Q/E — rotate camera left/right
  • Mouse drag — look around
  • Get close enough to a cube, then click it to collect

I added a simple game to my three.js world, including and instructions dropdown and WASD + QE movement.

    Once the game has been won, I change the sky box and sphere textures, as well as swapping out the cat obj to a sleeping cat obj, so it can take a nap too! I also implemented the addition camera view because it's cool to see and it helps with playing the game. You can adjust the three different lights as well!
